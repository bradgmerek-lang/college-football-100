HOW THE POOL WORKS

College Football 100 Rules

First to 100 correct picks on one active run wins. One wrong pick ends the run.

Official pool rules
1

Road to 100

Your goal is to reach 100 correct picks on a single active run. Correct picks add one win to that run. A new entry starts at zero.

2

Choose your games

You may pick any loaded FBS games, subject to the commissioner’s minimum, maximum, and spread requirements for that week. You do not have to pick every game.

3

Picks stay completely private

Before a game kicks off, other players cannot see your selection — or even tell that you made a selection in that game. No hidden-pick marker or future-pick total is exposed.

4

Each game locks at kickoff

You may add, change, or remove your pick until that game’s scheduled kickoff. At kickoff, the selection locks automatically and can no longer be changed by the player.

5

Picks reveal after kickoff

Once an individual game kicks off, that selection may appear in the player’s revealed picks. Games that have not started remain completely invisible to everyone else.

6

One loss ends the run

An incorrect pick ends the active run immediately for scoring purposes. Picks from games that kicked off earlier still count. Picks with a later kickoff time in that same week are void and do not count as wins or losses.

7

Same-time kickoffs are graded together

If multiple selected games have the exact same kickoff time, they are treated as the same scoring group. A loss in that group does not erase the results of the other games in that same group.

8

Re-entry starts the next week

If re-entry is allowed, a player who loses cannot start another run on the remaining games that week. The earliest possible new entry is the following week, subject to the season’s re-entry policy.

9

Opening spreads & weekly spread requirements

The site uses the opening spread captured for each game. A rule such as “at least 1 pick with a maximum favorite spread of 9.5” means at least one selected team must have a line of -9.5 or better. For example, -9.5, -7, +3, or +14 qualify; -10 or -14 do not.

10

Commissioner administration

The commissioner may correct imported or historical picks, player records, and grading when necessary. Administrative overrides are separate from normal player access and are recorded by the site’s audit tools.

Weekly settings can change.The “Current Week” panel above reflects the active week’s requirements. When a week has a commissioner override, those settings take precedence over the season defaults.
